## TYPOGRAPHY

### 4 Voices

| Voice | Font | Style | Size | Usage |
|-------|------|-------|------|-------|
| Category Title Large | Jersey 10 | ALL CAPS | 36px | Top-level nav, section names |
| Category Title Small | Jersey 10 | ALL CAPS | 22px | Sub-sections, sidebar labels |
| Subtitle | VT323 | Mixed case | 18px | Metadata, timestamps |
| Statement Title | Space Grotesk 700 | Normal | 22px, tracking -0.02em | Headlines, card titles |
| Body | Space Grotesk 400 | Normal | 15px | Paragraph text |
| Pixel | VT323 | Mixed case | 15px, letter-spacing 0.08em | System overlays |
| Mono | JetBrains Mono | Normal | 12px | Paths, sizes, timestamps |

## RULES

<div class="panel" style={{ display: 'flex', flexDirection: 'column', gap: '16px' }}>
  <div class="t-body">
    <strong>No gradients, no blur, no border-radius</strong> except on avatars (pill).
  </div>
  <div class="divider"></div>
  <div class="t-body">
    <strong>No emoji, no rounded chips, no soft anything.</strong>
  </div>
  <div class="divider"></div>
  <div class="t-body">
    <strong>Photos are sacred</strong> — no rounding, no tint, no filters on real imagery.
  </div>
  <div class="divider"></div>
  <div class="t-body">
    <strong>Only two legal shadows:</strong> Hard offset (5px 5px 0 0 var(--ink)) for panels and cards. Dithered offset (4×4 checker SVG behind the element) for hero moments only.
  </div>
  <div class="divider"></div>
  <div class="t-body">
    <strong>Pixelart icons</strong> for all icons, 24×24, 1px stroke.
  </div>
  <div class="divider"></div>
  <div class="t-body">
    <strong>Sentence case everywhere</strong> except Jersey 10 titles which are always ALL CAPS.
  </div>
</div>
